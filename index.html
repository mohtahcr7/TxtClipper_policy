Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how TxtClipper ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play (package: com.m77med.txtclipper).

This policy was last updated on May 29, 2025 and applies to all versions of the App published after this date. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

2
Information We Do NOT Collect

TxtClipper does not collect, store, process, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID)
  • Location data (GPS, IP address, or network location)
  • Text content you enter or paste into the App
  • Usage statistics or in-app behavior data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Financial or payment information
  • Photos, files, or media
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Any other personal or sensitive information
3
Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The only data stored by TxtClipper is your theme preference (Dark or Light Mode). This setting:

  • Is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (SharedPreferences)
  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not associated with any identity or account
  • Can be reset at any time by clearing app data in Android Settings
  • Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App
4
Clipboard Access

TxtClipper provides a "Paste" button that reads text from your device clipboard only when you explicitly tap it. This access:

  • Only occurs on explicit user action (tapping the Paste button)
  • Processes pasted text locally on your device only
  • Never stores, logs, or transmits clipboard content anywhere
  • Does NOT monitor your clipboard in the background
5
Permissions Requested

TxtClipper requests zero sensitive Android permissions. The App does NOT request:

  • INTERNET permission
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • READ_CONTACTS or any other contact-related permissions
  • CAMERA or RECORD_AUDIO
  • Any billing or in-app purchase permissions

Clipboard read/write on Android does not require a declared manifest permission for user-initiated actions (Android 10+).

6
Third-Party Services

TxtClipper does not integrate with any third-party services. The following are NOT present:

  • Analytics platforms (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Advertising SDKs (AdMob, Meta Audience Network, etc.)
  • Crash reporting tools (Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag, etc.)
  • Social media SDKs (Facebook SDK, Twitter SDK, etc.)
  • Cloud storage or sync services
  • Payment processors
  • Any external API calls whatsoever
7
Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. There is nothing to share. We will never sell, rent, lease, or provide user data to any external entity for any purpose.

8
Data Security

The best security is not collecting data in the first place. Since TxtClipper collects no user data and operates with no server infrastructure, there is zero risk of a server-side data breach exposing your information. The only local data (your theme preference) is non-sensitive and poses no privacy risk.

9
Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TxtClipper is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any users. Because we collect no data at all, TxtClipper fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar child privacy regulations worldwide.

Your Rights

Because TxtClipper collects no personal data:
